PermCalc

Estimate how fast a gas-charged, O-ring-sealed compartment loses pressure to permeation through the elastomer, and how long until it decays to a lockout (minimum functional) pressure.

Inputs

Gas
Compartment
O-rings

A compartment can be sealed by more than one O-ring (redundant seals, separate ports, different compounds) — add one card per O-ring; their permeation losses add together. A thicker cord (d2) means a longer diffusion path and less permeation; a wider contact band means more exposed area and more permeation.

How this is calculated

Estimation tool for engineering screening purposes — not a substitute for manufacturer permeation test data or a qualified seal design review. See README.md for the full derivation, assumptions and unit conventions. All calculations run locally in your browser; no data is sent anywhere.
